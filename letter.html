My Dearest Sayali,

There are not enough words in any language to tell you how much you mean to me. I have looked for the right ones for a long time now — in poems, in songs, in the quiet moments just before sleep — and I always come back to the same truth: that no arrangement of letters could ever capture what you are to me.

You are the person who makes ordinary mornings feel like gifts. The one who can fill a room with light simply by walking into it. The one whose laugh — that genuine, unguarded laugh — is quite possibly the most beautiful sound in my world. I don't deserve to hear it every day, but I am so grateful that I do.

I have watched you carry yourself through every kind of day with a grace that takes my breath away. Through the hard ones, you are still kind. Through the uncertain ones, you are still brave. Through the ordinary ones, you still find little ways to make everything feel lighter. That is who you are — and it is extraordinary.

Thank you for choosing me. For every morning you smiled at me, for every quiet evening we shared, for every time you held my hand without me asking. For every memory in this little universe I built for you tonight — you deserve to know that each one of them is precious to me, and that the best ones are still ahead of us.

My wish for you today and every day is simple: that you feel as loved as you truly are. That you see yourself the way I see you — radiant, strong, endlessly beautiful, and so very worthy of every wonderful thing this world has to offer.

Happy birthday, my prettiest bebe. I am so grateful you were born into this world. I am so grateful our paths found each other. And I am so deeply, endlessly grateful for the beautiful future still waiting for us.

With all my love, every single star in this universe,
and every tomorrow we have left,
Your Husband ❤️

Always yours 💛
